## Speaker

The speaker(s) you select will appear in the plot and analysis. The full pipeline of analysis is computed only on the selected speaker(s). When you change your selection, the processing is recomputed to reflect the current selection. When multiple speakers are selected, all of their data is pooled together. 

The speaker list is populated by the values in the `speaker_id` column of all uploaded datasets. For most files, there will be just one speaker per file. However, if you upload a dataset that has multiple speakers, they should still show up separately here.
